        How to play

        Each prompt has many right answers. Name one. The fewer people who would say it, the more it is worth.

        Every right answer moves you on from where you are. A rare answer skips stops on the way.

          Streaks. Every third right answer in a row gives a boost: one extra stop and 25 extra points.

          Misses. A wrong answer, a repeat or a timeout scores nothing. You stay where you are and your streak resets.

          Endless. Never ends and misses cost nothing. Past the last charted stop, every new place is generated: planets, stars, nebulas and galaxies in Space, and strange new zones in the Ocean. Tap End run when you want to see your results.

          No repeats. Once you have completed a question it does not come back until you have done all the others in that world. The Daily is the exception, because it has to be the same for everyone.

          Rules
          Rankings

          After each run you see what share of the other players you beat, for today, the last 7 days, the last 30 days or all time. It compares your score with each other player's best score in that period.

          The estimated crowd line is a model, not real people. It imagines 3,000 players who each give answers as often as the charts say people do.

          The share shown for each answer is a hand estimate of how many players out of 100 would give it.
